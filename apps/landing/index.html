Why your money is safe

  • No owner key. The contracts have no owner: Tazor cannot change them, take a payment or send it elsewhere.
  • Our relayer can only refuse. It cannot change who is paid or how much: the customer's signature fixes both.
  • Anyone can submit. A signed payment works without Tazor's servers; anyone can send it.
  • Verified contracts. TazorCheckout and TazorDepositCheckout, tested against the real Whitechain USDT.
