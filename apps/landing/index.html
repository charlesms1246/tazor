USDT checkout for businesses. Your customers pay without gas.

Scroll ↓

Your customers hold USDT. They shouldn’t need gas to spend it.

On most chains a buyer needs a second token just to pay you. A refund is a new transfer, and the books are rebuilt by hand.

Tazor is USDT checkout on Whitechain: the customer signs once, Tazor pays the gas, refunds go back to the payer, and the books come straight from the chain.

A

Pays without gas

The customer signs one message and holds 0 WBT. Tazor’s relayer pays the gas.

B

Refunds to the payer only

The merchant signs the refund; the contract sends it only to the customer who paid.

C

Books from the chain

Every payment, refund and charge as a dated CSV row, linked to its transaction.

Why your money is safe.

  • No owner key

    The contracts have no owner: Tazor cannot change them, take a payment or send it elsewhere.

  • Our relayer can only refuse

    It cannot change who is paid or how much: the customer’s signature fixes both.

  • Anyone can submit

    A signed payment works without Tazor’s servers; anyone can send it.

  • Verified contracts

    TazorCheckout ↗ and TazorDepositCheckout ↗, tested against the real Whitechain USDT.

More ways to get paid.

  • Customers sign once; you charge each cycle; they cancel any time, with one signature.

  • Each order gets its own deposit address. Any extra, or a late transfer, goes back to the customer.

  • Agents pay in USDT per API call; we run the facilitator for Whitechain.

  • Company payments need approvers with passkeys and roles on ENS. WB Soul verification is simulated on testnet.
