Send a signed payment through Ethereum

For when Tazor's site or servers are unavailable. This page is one file: save it and open it from your own computer, or host it anywhere. It talks only to your wallet and to Whitechain's public RPC; nothing goes to Tazor.

On Tazor's Pay page, after approving: "Send via Ethereum" → "Copy signed payment". It starts with tazor-signed-payment:1:

2. Check it

Ethereum checks that the approvers hold the approver role on the company's ENS name, then forwards the payment to Whitechain, which checks their signatures. If anything was changed, Whitechain refuses the payment and nothing is paid.

3. Send it from your wallet (Ethereum Sepolia)

Any Sepolia account with a little test ETH can send it; it doesn't have to be yours. You only pay the Ethereum gas.
